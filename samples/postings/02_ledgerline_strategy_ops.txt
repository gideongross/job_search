Strategy & Operations Lead — Ledgerline (NYSE: LDGR)
New York, NY · Hybrid

Ledgerline builds AI agents that automate regulatory compliance for banks and insurers — from
monitoring rule changes to drafting regulatory filings. Our models are deployed at more than 200
financial institutions. Ledgerline is a publicly traded company headquartered in New York.

About the role
Reporting to the Chief Operating Officer, you'll join a small Strategy & Operations team that runs
the company's planning process and leads cross-functional strategic initiatives.

Responsibilities
- Run the annual and quarterly planning process and the executive operating cadence
- Lead 2–3 strategic initiatives at a time (e.g. entering a new regulatory vertical, go-to-market
  expansion, operating model changes)
- Build business cases and financial models to support executive decisions
- Partner with Finance, Product, and Sales leadership to track company goals and KPIs
- Prepare board and executive materials

Qualifications
- 6+ years of experience in strategy consulting, corporate strategy, or BizOps
- Strong financial modeling and analytical skills (Excel/Sheets; SQL a plus)
- Track record leading cross-functional programs to completion
- Experience in financial services, compliance, or regulated industries preferred
- Bachelor's degree required; MBA or MPP a plus

Pay
Base salary range: $165,000 – $195,000. Eligible for annual bonus and RSUs.
