Deployment Strategist — Halden Systems
New York, NY (Hybrid, 3 days/week in our Flatiron office)

About Halden
Halden Systems builds AI software that helps the U.S. defense industrial base plan production and
manage supplier risk. Our platform uses machine learning models to forecast part shortages and
recommend mitigation across thousands of suppliers. We're a Series C company backed by leading
defense and enterprise investors, working with the Army, Navy and prime contractors.

The role
Deployment Strategists are the bridge between our customers' hardest operational problems and our
product. You'll embed with program offices and manufacturing customers, scope their problems, design
the deployment, and drive adoption with senior stakeholders. You will not write code — you'll work
alongside our Forward Deployed Engineers, who own the technical integration.

What you'll do
- Own the end-to-end deployment of Halden at 3–5 customer accounts
- Run discovery with program managers, supply-chain leads and executives; translate needs into
  deployment plans and success metrics
- Lead change management and executive business reviews
- Feed customer insight to product and help shape the roadmap
- Support proposal and contracting work for new government programs

What we're looking for
- 5+ years in management consulting, strategy & operations, or customer deployment roles
- Experience working with government, defense or industrial customers
- Excellent written and executive communication
- Comfort with data: SQL or spreadsheet analysis is a plus
- Ability to obtain a U.S. security clearance

Compensation
The base salary range for this role in New York is $170,000–$210,000, plus equity and benefits.
Travel to customer sites up to 25%.
