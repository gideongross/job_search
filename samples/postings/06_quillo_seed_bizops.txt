Founding BizOps Lead — Quillo
New York, NY (in-person, SoHo)

Quillo is building AI copilots for procurement teams at mid-sized manufacturers. We're a 6-person
team that just closed a $3M seed round led by top-tier investors, and we're hiring our first
business hire.

You'll work directly with our two co-founders on everything that isn't engineering: customer
onboarding, pricing, finance, hiring, and investor updates. This is a true generalist role — expect
to wear many hats.

What you'll do
- Own customer onboarding for our first 10 design partners
- Set up our finance, legal and HR operations
- Build our pricing model and sales pipeline tracking
- Help prepare for our Series A raise

You might be a fit if
- You have 3+ years in consulting, BizOps or operations at a startup
- You're comfortable with ambiguity and scrappy execution
- You're excited about manufacturing and supply chain

Base salary: $120,000 – $135,000 + meaningful equity (0.5–1.0%).
