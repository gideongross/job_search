Strategic Initiatives Manager — Arbiter AI
Remote (United States)

Arbiter AI builds large-language-model software that helps state and local governments process
permits and benefits applications faster. We're a Series B company serving 40+ public agencies.

We are a fully remote company with no offices; the team gathers in person twice a year.

About the role
Reporting to the Chief of Staff, you'll lead high-priority strategic initiatives across the business:
public-sector go-to-market planning, partnership strategy with systems integrators, and internal
operating improvements.

You will
- Scope and lead cross-functional initiatives from problem definition through execution
- Build business cases, market sizing and financial models
- Drive the quarterly OKR process and exec reporting
- Coordinate with state and local government partners on pilot programs

You have
- 5+ years in strategy consulting, strategy & operations, or government
- Experience selling to or working in the public sector
- Excellent writing and executive presence

Compensation: $160,000 – $190,000 base (U.S. national range) plus equity.
