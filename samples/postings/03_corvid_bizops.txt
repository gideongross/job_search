Business Operations Manager — Corvid Freight
New York, NY (in office, Monday–Thursday)

Corvid Freight is a digital freight brokerage connecting shippers with carriers across North America.
We use technology and machine learning to price loads and match trucks, and our marketplace moves
thousands of shipments every week.

We're looking for a Business Operations Manager to help our brokerage team scale. You'll sit in the
operations org and work with carrier sales, pricing and customer success.

What you'll do
- Own weekly operating metrics for the brokerage floor (margin per load, tender acceptance, on-time %)
- Identify process bottlenecks and run improvement projects
- Build dashboards and ad-hoc analyses in SQL and Excel
- Support carrier sales leadership with territory planning and headcount models
- Manage special projects for the VP of Operations

What you bring
- 3–5 years in operations, BizOps, or consulting, ideally in logistics or a marketplace business
- Strong SQL and Excel skills
- Comfort in a fast-paced, high-volume environment
- Freight brokerage experience strongly preferred

Competitive salary and benefits. Some evening coverage during peak season.
