Forward Deployed Engineer — Vantor
New York, NY — Hybrid (3 days in office)

Vantor builds an AI platform that helps energy utilities predict equipment failures and optimize
grid maintenance. We raised a $90M Series C last year and serve 30 utilities across the U.S.

As a Forward Deployed Engineer you'll work on-site with utility customers to integrate Vantor with
their data systems and build custom workflows on top of our platform.

Responsibilities
- Write production-quality Python and TypeScript to build customer-specific data pipelines and
  applications
- Integrate with customer SCADA, asset-management and GIS systems via APIs
- Debug and deploy models in customer cloud environments (AWS, Azure)
- Partner with customers' operations teams to scope and prioritize use cases

Requirements
- BS in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field (required)
- 3+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL; experience with TypeScript/React a plus
- Experience with cloud infrastructure and data engineering tools (Spark, Airflow)
- Willingness to travel to customer sites 30%

Base salary range (New York): $175,000 – $220,000 plus equity.
